Before you start

  1. Sit still for five minutes. Feet anchored (in zero gravity, hook them under a rail), arm resting at heart level.
  2. Rest one fingertip lightly on the pulse sensor. Keep the hand still and relaxed. Pressing hard spoils the reading.
  3. Put the temperature probe under your arm and keep the arm down, ideally ten minutes before the check.
  4. Have a cuff or glucometer? You can type their readings during the check.

Why it matters in space. Without gravity, fluid shifts toward your head and the heart gets lazier. That shows up as a higher resting pulse, blood-pressure changes and dizziness on landing. Radiation also adds long-term heart risk.

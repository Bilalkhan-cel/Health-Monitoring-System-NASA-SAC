Built for when Earth is 22 minutes away.

  • Local-first

    Vitals, camera and microphone analysis run on this computer. The wearable talks only to it, over your own Wi-Fi. Only Dr. NOVA's voice uses the cloud.

    Video is never saved. Voice recordings are deleted once they're read. Transcripts are off by default.

    A prototype, not a medical device. It never diagnoses, and it tells you which numbers were measured if you ask.
